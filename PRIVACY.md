# Privacy policy: Reel Ad Blocker for Facebook

**The extension collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.**

- It runs only on `facebook.com` pages and reads the reel on screen (its text and links) in your
  browser, to decide whether it is an ad.
- Ads you mark are saved as "rules" (advertiser name, destination domains, a hash of the caption) in the
  browser's local extension storage, on your device only. Export, import and deletion are on the
  extension's manage page; uninstalling the extension removes them.
- It makes no network requests, has no analytics or tracking, and uses no remote code.
- The only permission it asks for is `storage`, to keep your rules and settings.

Meta Platforms, Inc., used here only to say which website the extension works on.
